Add Scott’s Yard to your Home Screen. Open straight into Plants, with your last selected view remembered on this device.

Use the instructions below to add the app to your Home Screen.

Open Plants →

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open satownsend.com/app/ in Safari on your phone.
  2. Open the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. If it is missing, look under Edit Actions.
  3. Leave Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add.
  4. Open Scott’s Yard from your Home Screen.

On Android or a computer

If an Install app button appears above, use it. Otherwise, look for Install app or Add to Home screen in your browser’s menu. Available options depend on your browser.

Sign in once inside the app

In Plants, open Settings → Account & storage and choose Sign in with Google. Finish Google’s sign-in and return to the app. The installed app may need its own sign-in even if Safari is already signed in. Your existing Sheets data, photos and editing permissions stay the same.

Made for everyday use

The phone navigation bar puts Plants, Quick log, Care and Weather within reach. Use the Plants search to find a record; its existing photo upload supports your phone’s image picker. All other collections stay available in the site menu.

This is the live website in an app window. An internet connection is required for live data, syncing, photo uploads and sign-in. Updates arrive through the website; there is no separate app-store release to install.
