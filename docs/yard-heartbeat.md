# The yard’s heartbeat (#199)

Open `/yard-3d/#heartbeat`, select a year, and use Play year, Pause/Resume, Restart year, the date slider, Jump to date, Previous/Next day, or 0.25×–4× speed. One complete selected timeline takes roughly 60 seconds at 1×. Playback starts paused. A current-year timeline ends at today in America/New_York and is labeled year so far; its full-year calendar denominator is shown separately. Leap years retain February 29. Dates before the weather archive began and unavailable recent weather remain gaps.

`yard-3d/timeline.js` is a reusable calendar/playback component. It accepts visible-frame deltas instead of elapsed wall time, supports seeking and speed changes, stops at the end, and restarts on replay. The scene pauses when its stage leaves the viewport or the document becomes hidden. It never catches up through unseen records after a hidden tab resumes. Reduced-motion mode starts paused like the normal view and uses static precipitation/ripple markers even if the visitor explicitly starts date playback.

## Sources and interpretation

- Daily ERA5 temperature and total precipitation use `weatherYear(year, {preserveMissing:true})`. Zero precipitation is a known modeled dry day; missing precipitation or temperature stays unavailable. Precipitation includes rain and snow water equivalent. Particle appearance is illustrative and does not identify observed precipitation type or measured amount.
- Ripples correspond to exact `watered` journal actions on the selected date. Other care records are available in the paused list. No water volume is inferred. Missing journal rows do not establish no care occurred.
- Seasonal canopy colors use expected monthly windows from current AI plant overviews. They are not observations or historical bloom dates. An absent expected window is neutral, not an assertion that a plant was bare. The schematic garden uses the current catalog, including historical/dead catalog entries; positions, sizes and presence in a historical year are not measured or reconstructed.
- Wildlife tokens correspond to the selected date's recorded sightings. Neutral paw symbols do not invent animal species. Species, locations, notes and source sighting links are listed while paused. No sighting is not evidence of absence.
- Dated photo markers follow the shared photo catalog. Paused records open permitted original media and load thumbnails lazily; playback does not fetch a new image texture every day. Hidden photos, wishlist/noncatalog plants and signed-out yard photos are excluded. If inventory visibility metadata is unavailable, unverifiable plant photos are withheld. Authentication changes refresh the permitted photo index.
- Source errors are explicit and distinct from a successfully loaded catalog with no records. The scene can still show the other sources when one fails. Retry weather reloads the archive. Reload the scene/page to re-read failed nonweather sources.

The paused HTML companion includes original journal links, source dashboards, all current-day records, expected color windows, separate weather coverage counts and a visible date. Scrollable lists are keyboard focusable. Notes are escaped. No causal explanations, generated observations or AI requests are produced. This synchronized playback complements the existing #97 annual recap rather than replacing its photo curation.

## Rendering and verification

Plant trunks/crowns use two instanced meshes. Precipitation is bounded at 256 particles for a narrow/coarse-pointer device and 640 elsewhere. A frame shows at most 32 ripple effects, eight neutral wildlife tokens and one photo marker; the paused lists and readout preserve the full record counts. Ring geometry is single-sided toward the allowed above-ground camera to avoid a redundant transparent draw pass. There are no per-frame geometry allocations or live photo-texture downloads. The shared stage already suspends rendering while hidden/offscreen.

`tests/heartbeat.cjs` validates 60-second timing, pause/resume/replay/speed/scrubbing, leap/current years, source date alignment, seasonal windows, missing-vs-zero values, future exclusion and photo privacy. Browser checks cover playback, paused records, keyboard scrubbing, year selection, reduced motion, failed sources, escaped notes and scene cleanup. A temporary 390px frame fixture stresses 256 plants, 40 watering records, 16 sightings and heavy precipitation, checks overflow and measures render submission time. The stress fixture measured 59 draw submissions, 256 precipitation points and about 51,500 triangles; sampled frame submission time had a median around 0.7 ms and a 95th percentile around 1.3 ms on the test host. These measurements use the desktop browser at phone width, not a physical-phone GPU benchmark. Temporary fixtures and telemetry are not published.
