    Google account & sync
    Plants, tags, photos, the log and the care calendar live in a Google Sheet. Anyone can view; sign in to edit. Changes sync row by row a couple of seconds after you make them.
    Sync: Read-only (not signed in).
    Data: Not loaded yet.
    Photos: Not configured.
    Advanced: photo storage keys (Cloudflare R2)
    Bucket plant-photos · public URL pub-59a9261…r2.dev. Keys are entered once while signed in and kept in a private Google vault, so any other device loads them on sign-in. You only need this to rotate keys or rebuild photo rows.
    Backup & print
    The Google Sheet is the source of truth. Export writes a JSON snapshot of everything on this page (plants, log, care calendar, settings) as a second backup. Print builds a clean inventory for paper or PDF.
    Troubleshooting
    If this browser looks out of date, reload from the sheet. Nothing is deleted; anything added here but not yet synced is kept.
