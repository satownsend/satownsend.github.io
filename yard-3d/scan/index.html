How to turn yard photos into a scan

Capture a slow walk around and through the yard, with sharp, overlapping views of each tree, bed and building. Use one session in calm, even light. Photos from different seasons should be separate scans.

Reconstruct with COLMAP + Nerfstudio Splatfacto, or export a Gaussian splat from a capture app. Clean away floating artifacts in SuperSplat and export a bundled SPZ, SOG or PLY. Open that file here. Ordinary photos and an aerial map cannot reveal unseen sides of the yard.
